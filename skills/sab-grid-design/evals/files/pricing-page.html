What every plan includes

All plans share the same ingestion pipeline, role-based access, and export API. Limits differ only in retention, seats, and support response time. Annual billing saves two months; invoices can be paid by card or bank transfer.

Usage is metered per active dashboard editor. Viewers are free on every plan, so sharing a read-only dashboard with the whole company does not change the bill.

Need a custom data residency region or a signed DPA? Business plans include both; contact sales with your procurement timeline.
